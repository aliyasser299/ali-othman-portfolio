# Design direction

The supplied `portfolioDESIGN.md` is the main visual reference. Its design tokens were adapted to a personal creative portfolio: warm Pumice canvas, Limestone surfaces, Ember actions, one Plasma Violet halftone signature, Sulfur metadata badges, condensed architectural headings, DM Sans Medium body copy, flat surfaces, generous radii, and dotted rules.

The document’s example statistics, partner strips, crypto positioning, and announcement components were visual examples rather than portfolio content requirements. The site uses the owner’s actual work and supplied contact details.

## Research and adaptation

- [Carson Fidje’s Behance portfolio study](https://www.behance.net/gallery/244043479/Personal-Website): personal identity, clear hierarchy, and a portfolio treated as a coherent visual system.
- [Damon Tsang’s graphic designer portfolio study](https://www.behance.net/gallery/247891583/a-Graphic-Designers-Portfolio-Website): a restrained layout that allows individual work to remain distinct.
- [Awwwards: Takafumi Senda Portfolio](https://www.awwwards.com/sites/takafumi-senda-portfolio): clean gallery presentation and responsive creative direction.
- [Andrei Trusov’s video editing portfolio](https://andreitrusov.com/): work-first organization and concise creative positioning.
- [Adam Allegrucci’s editing and motion portfolio](https://www.adamallegrucci.com/): short role descriptions and a direct route into selected work.
- [Studio Dumbar](https://studiodumbar.com/): visual identities and motion presented as the work itself.

These informed presentation patterns; no layouts, artwork, copy, or project claims were copied.

## Information architecture

1. Ali Y. Othman → Graphic Designer & Video Editor → concise creative statement.
2. A cinematic featured trailer, a horizontally browsable portrait collection, and a landscape motion grid.
3. Category filters for the content actually available: Video Editing, Social Content, Motion Graphics.
4. A brief about section and four relevant capabilities.
5. Direct email, WhatsApp, and supplied social links.

The hero’s play control opens the actual personal identity animation. It is not labeled as a showreel. All imagery comes from supplied videos; the halftone and favicon are original code-based brand graphics. No external stock assets, fabricated clients, awards, or experience statistics are used.

Portrait videos preserve both the 9:16 and 17:30 source formats; the trailer preserves its cinematic widescreen format. Every viewing surface contains rather than crops the video. Mobile layouts stack the hero and about content, simplify the motion grid, and expose portrait projects through a touch-scrollable rail with keyboard support and navigation buttons.
